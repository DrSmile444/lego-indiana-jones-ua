title: Feedback

---

The translation is complete, but the game is big, and something may still be hiding in a corner of a level we never reached. Every report helps. You can write in English or Ukrainian.

## Where to write

- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DrSmile444/lego-indiana-jones-ua/issues)** — best for bugs and suggestions: they are public and nothing gets lost.
- **Telegram: [@dimkasmile](https://t.me/dimkasmile)** — if a messenger is easier for you.

## What to include

1. **What happened.** A typo, clipped or overlong text, a line still in English, boxes instead of letters, a game crash.
2. **Where.** The level and episode, or the menu or screen.
3. **A screenshot.** In Steam press `F12`; find the shots under “View → Screenshots”.
4. **A save file** — if the problem shows up in a specific place that takes a long time to reach.
5. **The localization version** (for example, 1.1.10) and the game version (Steam, GOG, Steam Deck).

## Translation suggestions

Have a better word or phrase? First check the [glossary](/lego-indiana-jones-ua/en/glossary/) and the [cultural adaptations](/lego-indiana-jones-ua/en/adaptations/): they explain why each option was chosen. If you still think it could be better, write to us and we’ll discuss it.
